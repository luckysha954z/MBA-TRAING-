Ethical Farming & 4-Step Cold Chain

Our Nagpur farm follows stress-free cattle care with shaded open paddocks, clean drinking water, mineral-balanced green fodder (maize, berseem, and dry roughage), and routine veterinary checkups—never synthetic hormones.

Every Morning Batch Logged

Fat %, SNF, temperature (≤ 4°C), and adulteration screening recorded prior to dispatch.

STEP 01

Morning Milking

Hygienic closed-line milking begins at 3:30 AM from well-rested, fodder-fed cows and buffaloes.

STEP 02

Chilling to 4°C

Rapid bulk-chilling drops milk temperature to 4°C immediately, preserving natural sweetness without preservatives.

STEP 03

Lab Quality Check

Each batch passes Fat, SNF, MBRT freshness, and zero-adulteration tests before glass or pouch sealing.

STEP 04

Insulated Doorstep Delivery

Chilled crates roll out across Nagpur neighborhoods for quiet doorstep delivery before 7:00 AM.
